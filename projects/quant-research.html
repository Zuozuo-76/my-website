Execution-Aware Multi-Factor Equity Research

执行约束下的多因子量化研究

An end-to-end study of 300 anonymized stocks across 302 trading days, designed to test what remains after t+1 execution, transaction costs, market impact, turnover controls, and honest sample-boundary labeling.

围绕 300 只匿名股票与 302 个交易日开展端到端研究，重点检验在 t+1 执行、交易成本、市场冲击、换手控制与诚实样本边界下仍能保留的结果。

01 / RESEARCH QUESTION

Can a factor signal survive realistic execution?

因子信号能否经受真实执行约束？

The study asks a practical question: after delaying execution to t+1 and charging fees, slippage, market impact, and turnover, does a cross-sectional equity signal retain useful risk-adjusted performance?

本研究关注一个实践问题：将执行推迟至 t+1，并计入手续费、滑点、市场冲击与换手后，横截面股票信号是否仍能保留有用的风险调整表现？

A separate LSTM experiment evaluates prediction under non-overlapping walk-forward test windows. Its classification metrics are reported independently and are not presented as trading returns.

独立的 LSTM 实验在互不重叠的 walk-forward 测试窗口中评估预测能力。其分类指标与交易收益分开报告，不作等价解释。

02 / DATA & PIPELINE

One traceable path from raw records to evidence.

从原始记录到研究证据的可追溯流程。

01

Prepare

数据准备

Clean tick data, handle outliers, aggregate minute and daily bars, and validate schemas before research.

清洗逐笔数据、处理异常值、聚合分钟与日频行情，并在研究前验证数据结构。

02

因子构建

Create cross-sectional signals with lag-aware features so the ranking step uses only information available at the decision time.

使用严格滞后的特征构建横截面信号，确保排序仅使用决策时点可得信息。

03

Execute

执行回测

Apply t+1 fills, portfolio buffers, turnover controls, costs, slippage, market impact, and volatility targeting.

纳入 t+1 成交、持仓缓冲、换手控制、交易费用、滑点、市场冲击与波动率目标。

04

Evaluate

评估

Separate full-sample strategy analysis, a selection-influenced holdout, and strict walk-forward LSTM test folds.

明确区分完整样本策略分析、参与版本选择的留出期，以及严格 walk-forward 的 LSTM 测试窗口。

04 / OOS FINDINGS

Strict OOS applies to prediction—not the entire strategy.

严格样本外仅适用于预测实验，而非整套策略。

The PyTorch LSTM uses three non-overlapping walk-forward test folds, producing 83,880 strict out-of-sample predictions and ROC AUC 0.6465.

PyTorch LSTM 使用三个互不重叠的 walk-forward 测试窗口，生成 83,880 条严格样本外预测，ROC AUC 为 0.6465。

Boundary:边界： these are classification results, not realized trading returns. The holdout strategy result remains quasi-OOS.这些是分类结果，不是已实现交易收益；策略留出期结果仍属于准样本外。
05 / LIMITATIONS

What this study does not prove.

这项研究尚不能证明什么。

01

Anonymized stocks prevent real industry and market-cap neutralization.

股票匿名化，无法开展真实行业与市值中性化。

02

The 302-day sample is too short to establish multi-cycle stability.

302 个交易日的样本不足以证明跨周期稳定性。

03

The final holdout influenced strategy selection, so it is not strict OOS.

最终留出期参与策略选择，因此不属于严格样本外。

04

Market impact is model-estimated rather than replayed from live fills.

市场冲击来自模型估计，并非实盘成交回放。

05

The LSTM experiment covers six stocks and 140 trading days; recall is 8.10% despite stronger ROC AUC.

LSTM 实验仅覆盖 6 只股票与 140 个交易日；虽然 ROC AUC 较高，但 Recall 仅 8.10%。

06

No multi-market or multi-period robustness study has been completed.

尚未完成多市场或多时间区间的稳健性检验。
